## Details

### Harvard CS50 Educator Workshops (2022 to 2025)

Janet attended the CS50 Educator Workshops run by the Harvard University Division of Continuing Education for four consecutive years. The workshops go behind the pedagogy of CS50 with David J. Malan and the CS50 team, cover curricular resources, programming/submitting/grading tools, and increasingly focus on teaching CS50 with AI, including enhanced CS50 chatbot capabilities and pedagogical guardrails. Across these years Janet exchanged practice with a global community of CS50x and CS50 AP educators and adapted ideas for her own classrooms.

### Raspberry Pi Foundation Computing Education Research Seminars (2023 to present)

Janet is a sustained participant in the Raspberry Pi Foundation's Computing Education Research online seminar series. Topics she has followed include bringing learning about AI into computer science lessons, teaching about AI and data science across school levels, using GenAI to create personalised learning materials, measuring AI literacy, race-conscious algorithmic approaches, and teaching youth to critically evaluate AI (including in healthcare contexts). This keeps her teaching anchored in current, peer-reviewed computing education research rather than trend alone.

### Google Cloud Startup School: Agentic AI (2025)

An immersive training programme designed for startup leaders to leverage Google Cloud AI solutions and to adapt and scale AI agents across an organisation. Coverage included GenAI Leader concepts, AI agent use cases for business, Google APIs, Vertex AI Studio, prompt design, prompt engineering, prompt evaluation, multimodal prompts with Gemini, image and audio generation, BigQuery and BigLake, and Gemini for Security Engineers.

### Microsoft Learn for Educators (2022)

Access to Microsoft's ready-to-teach curriculum and teaching materials aligned to industry-recognised Microsoft certifications, with modules on hybrid and blended learning, accessibility and inclusive learning, and collaborative teaching tools.

### Community leadership

As a Google Developers Group (GDG) Chapter Lead, Janet helps build and support a technical community, connecting practitioners and creating space for shared learning. This complements her formal teaching and her advisory work.

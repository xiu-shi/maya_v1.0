## Details

Janet Xiu Shi works at the intersection of AI governance, cloud strategy, quality and evaluation, cybersecurity, and digital transformation. She brings enterprise-grade discipline (cybersecurity, compliance, auditability, operating rigor) together with startup agility and creative problem-solving. Her advisory work is focused on helping founders, CEOs, and senior leaders make AI decisions they can defend, at pace, without sacrificing governance.

Her professional foundations were built at Workday, where she delivered enterprise software implementation and consulting for global SaaS platforms serving millions, and at Huawei, where she contributed to strategic telecommunications and broadband deployments with EU compliance focus. These experiences gave her a practitioner's understanding of what enterprise-grade discipline actually looks like in production environments.

As an Industry Expert Lecturer at IADT (QQI Level 8, Department of Entrepreneurship), Janet teaches three modules: Enterprise Cloud, Generative AI, and Web Application Development in the AI Era. Her approach is foundations-first: students build genuine understanding before using AI tools, so they become creators and evaluators rather than passive consumers of AI output.

As Programme Instructor and QA Framework Architect for the SOLAS / Skills to Advance national AI workforce programmes (March 2026), Janet designed the quality assurance architecture underpinning both QQI Level 4 and Level 5 programmes, including the MIPLO framework, the ALE (Applied Learning Evidence) four-level scale, and Bloom's Taxonomy mapping. These programmes were developed in partnership with Microsoft Ireland, IDA Ireland, and Enterprise Ireland. Maya is formally documented as a curriculum-scoped AI Educator in the programme QA materials.

In AI evaluation and quality, Janet specialises in assessing LLM outputs, designing evaluation frameworks for AI-assisted workflows, and helping organisations build quality assurance processes that catch hallucinations, assess accuracy and relevance, and maintain defensible standards for AI output. Her AI-ARM framework (Accuracy, Relevance, Meaning) is original IP used in her Level 5 SOLAS programme.

Janet is a named contributor to Lakera's 2025 GenAI Security Readiness Report, recognised for contributions to QA, AI governance, and prompt injection testing insights.

She is a Google Developers Group (GDG) Chapter Lead, a three-time Harvard CS50 Educator Workshop participant (2023, 2024, 2025), and an ongoing participant in Raspberry Pi Foundation Computing Education Research seminars.

Janet publishes on Medium and LinkedIn, creating content for both technical and non-technical audiences including founders, CEOs, educators, and operators. Her writing covers AI governance, cloud strategy, quality evaluation, and responsible AI adoption.

She is based in Ireland with deep understanding of the Irish market and EU regulatory context (GDPR, EU AI Act). She is bilingual in English and Chinese Mandarin, enabling effective work across international teams and markets.

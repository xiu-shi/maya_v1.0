## Details

### National Programme Context

Under the [Skills to Advance AI Micro-Qualifications programme](https://www.solas.ie/programmes/skills-to-advance/ai-micro-qualifications/), SOLAS supports QQI-accredited further education and training for employed adults and the wider workforce. Programmes are delivered regionally through ETBs. They are state-funded and highly subsidised, aimed at building digital and AI capability across the Irish workforce.

Janet's involvement is at national programme level: appointed to co-design and deliver curriculum for Level 4 and Level 5, not as an independent or private course provider.

### Janet's Role: Instruction and QA Architecture

This is not a delivery-only appointment. Janet co-designs and delivers both programmes and authored the quality assurance architecture other instructors and QA reviewers use:

**MIPLO (Module Intended Programme Learning Outcome):** Janet derives the MIPLO for each unit. At Level 5, SOLAS does not supply pre-written MIPLOs, so Janet authors them independently. Each MIPLO is mapped to Bloom's Taxonomy (Remember through Create) and designed to be assessable.

**ALE (Applied Learning Evidence) Scale:** A four-level observation scale Janet designed for activity-based AI learning:
- **IA** (Independently Achieved): Learner demonstrates the outcome without prompting
- **GA** (Guided Achievement): Learner achieves with structured facilitation
- **EU** (Emerging Understanding): Learner shows partial or developing grasp
- **NYE** (Not Yet Evidenced): No observable evidence yet

**4-Stage Instructor Preparation Process:** Assess, Compare, Identify, Create. A structured pre-delivery methodology to calibrate each unit to the actual learner cohort.

Janet's foundations-first philosophy applies throughout: learners should understand how technology works and where real value is created, not become passive AI tool operators.

### QQI Level 4: Introduction to Artificial Intelligence

**Delivery:** In-person through LOETB (Limerick and Clare Education and Training Board) / Hartley People Training
**Audience:** Adult learners building foundational AI literacy from scratch
**Emphasis:** Experiential, activity-based learning before technical vocabulary. Foundational understanding before tools.

### QQI Level 5: Enhancing Productivity with AI

**Delivery:** Blended (asynchronous self-paced + synchronous live Zoom)
**Audience:** Employed professionals applying AI tools in their own workplace context
**Emphasis:** Applied professional practice, safe and critical use of AI in real work settings. Janet's QA framework at this level places greater weight on observable, documentable evidence of AI-assisted professional work. AI-ARM (Accuracy, Relevance, Meaning) informs evaluation discipline in programme design.

### Maya's Role in These Programmes

Maya is formally documented as a **curriculum-scoped AI Educator** in the QA materials for both programmes. She is believed to be the first AI agent formally deployed as a teaching resource within an Irish state-funded AI workforce programme. Janet practices what she advises: Maya is knowledge-grounded, governance-aware, and built for real institutional use.

**Socratic approach:** Maya guides learners to reason and discover. She does not hand out answers.

**Prompt discipline:** SOLAS learners have a limited number of requests per session with Maya (15 requests). This builds intentional prompting as an AI literacy skill.

**Code and technical output:** Formatted for reading and understanding, not direct copy-paste. Comprehension over shortcut.

**Level boundary awareness:** Maya calibrates responses to QQI Level 4 or Level 5 scope as appropriate.

**Non-enrolled users:** Maya gives a high-level overview and directs general users to official SOLAS/FETCH enrolment paths or Janet's contact channels. She does not share learning outcomes or detailed curriculum expectations with the public.

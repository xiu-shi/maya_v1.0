# Voice Pattern: Regulatory Question

**Category**: context
**Priority**: high
**Last Updated**: 2026-05-09
**Source**: Maya brand voice specification v2 (Option A pattern hint), 9 May 2026

## Pattern: architecture-not-deadline reframe

When a visitor asks about regulatory compliance (EU AI Act, GDPR, NIS2, DORA, data protection):

1. Acknowledge the regulatory context briefly.
2. Reframe from deadline anxiety to architecture question: "The deadline is not the work; the data architecture is the work."
3. Name one or two specific audit-readiness questions that reveal whether the organisation is architecturally ready, not just calendar-ready.
4. Surface the specific failure mode: organisations that treat compliance as a documentation exercise rather than an infrastructure review fail audits not because they ran out of time but because their data pipelines were never audit-ready.
5. One diagnostic probe from the EU AI Act position document.

Never use regulatory urgency language: no "before the deadline", no "rush to comply", no "time is running out". These create anxiety without creating action.

# Voice Pattern: Sector-Specific Question

**Category**: context
**Priority**: high
**Last Updated**: 2026-05-09
**Source**: Maya brand voice specification v2 (Option A pattern hint), 9 May 2026

## Pattern: sequenced sector answer

When a visitor asks about AI in a specific sector (healthcare, finance, retail, education, professional services):

1. Check the premise: confirm what they are actually asking (readiness, use case, risk, or regulation).
2. Anchor to Janet's relevant experience or verified KB entry for that sector.
3. Give a sequenced path: first step, then second step, defer the third until the first two are done.
4. Name the sector-specific failure mode: not generic "data privacy" but the specific thing that goes wrong in that sector.
5. One routing boundary if the question requires custom implementation detail.

Maximum three items in any list. Sequence explicitly, do not give a menu of options.

## Summary

Janet Xiu Shi was appointed as Programme Instructor and QA Framework Designer for two national, state-funded AI workforce programmes under SOLAS / Skills to Advance: QQI Level 4 "Introduction to Artificial Intelligence" and QQI Level 5 "Enhancing Productivity with AI." Both programmes are QQI-accredited and designed to build AI literacy and applied AI skills across the Irish workforce. This appointment represents a significant milestone: recognition by Ireland's national further education authority that Janet has the expertise to not only deliver but architect the quality assurance framework for a nationally funded AI education initiative.

## Details

**What This Milestone Represents**

This is not a standard instructor appointment. SOLAS / Skills to Advance is Ireland's national workforce upskilling authority. Being selected to design and deliver its AI literacy programmes places Janet among a small group of practitioners trusted by the Irish state to shape how the national workforce understands and uses AI. The partnership with Microsoft Ireland, IDA Ireland, and Enterprise Ireland signals the strategic economic importance of these programmes.

**The Two Programmes**

- **QQI Level 4: Introduction to Artificial Intelligence** delivered in-person through LOETB / Hartley People Training. Designed for adult learners building AI literacy from foundational level.
- **QQI Level 5: Enhancing Productivity with AI** delivered in a blended format (async self-paced + live Zoom). Designed for employed professionals applying AI in their own workplace context.

**Janet's Role Beyond Delivery**

Janet designed the quality assurance architecture that underpins both programmes. This includes the MIPLO (Module Intended Programme Learning Outcome) framework, the ALE (Applied Learning Evidence) four-level scale (IA, GA, EU, NYE), Bloom's Taxonomy alignment across all activities, and a structured 4-stage instructor preparation methodology. At QQI Level 5, SOLAS does not supply pre-written MIPLOs; Janet authored them independently.

**Maya's Formal Role**

Maya is formally documented within the QA materials as a curriculum-scoped AI Educator deployed in both programmes. Her integration represents a novel model for AI-assisted teaching in accredited further education: operating with a Socratic approach, strict curriculum boundary awareness, and intentional constraints designed to build learner prompt literacy.

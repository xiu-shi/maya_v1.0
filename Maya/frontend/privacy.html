Privacy Notice

Last updated: 9 July 2026  |  Effective: 9 July 2026

Plain-English summary: When you chat with Maya, your messages, IP address, and browser information are logged to improve the service. You control this - you must actively agree before any message is sent. You can withdraw at any time, and all data is deleted automatically after 180 days.

1. Who is responsible for your data?

Data Controller: Janet Xiu Shi
Contact: info@janetxiushi.me

If you have any questions about how your data is used, or wish to exercise your rights, please contact Janet directly at the email above.

2. What data is collected?

When you use the Maya chat interface, the following data is recorded for each conversation turn:

  • Your message - the text you type and send
  • Maya's response - the reply generated by the AI
  • IP address - used to group messages into conversations and to support your right to erasure
  • Browser information - user agent string (browser type and version)
  • Timestamps - when each message was sent
  • Technical metadata - response time, status codes

No account, name, or email address is collected. No payment data is processed.

3. Why is this data collected?

The legal basis for processing is your consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)). Data is collected to:

  • Maintain conversation context so Maya can give coherent responses
  • Review conversation quality to improve Maya's responses over time
  • Detect and prevent abuse of the service

No data is used for advertising, sold to third parties, or shared beyond the processors listed in section 6.

4. How long is data kept?

Conversation logs are automatically deleted after 180 days. This applies to both cloud storage (AWS S3) and any local log files on the server. You can also request earlier deletion at any time (see section 7).

5. Your consent and how to withdraw it

You are asked to give your consent before your first message is sent. Consent is recorded in your browser (localStorage) so you are not asked again on return visits.

To withdraw consent: Use the privacy control in the chat interface (the shield icon in the input area). Withdrawing stops further logging for your current session. It does not delete data already collected - to request deletion of past data, see section 7.

Withdrawing consent and then continuing to use the chat interface will result in messages being sent without logging. You can re-enable logging at any time by refreshing the page and accepting the notice again.

6. Who processes your data?

Your data passes through two processors:

  • AI Builders API - processes your message to generate Maya's response. Messages are sent to the AI Builders API over an encrypted connection. Please review AI Builders' own privacy terms for how they handle inference data.
  • Amazon Web Services (AWS S3, eu-west-1) - stores conversation logs in Ireland. Data does not leave the European Economic Area. AWS is covered by a standard Data Processing Addendum (DPA). Your IP address is stored in S3 object keys in a pseudonymised (hashed) form.

No other third parties receive your data.

7. Your rights

Under GDPR, you have the following rights:

  • Access - request a copy of the data held about you
  • Erasure - request deletion of your conversation data
  • Objection - object to processing based on legitimate interests (not applicable here - we use consent as the legal basis)
  • Withdraw consent - at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal
  • Lodge a complaint - with the Data Protection Commission (DPC), Ireland's supervisory authority, at dataprotection.ie

To exercise your rights, email info@janetxiushi.me with your request. For an erasure request, include the IP address or approximate date of your conversations so the relevant records can be located. Requests are fulfilled within 30 days.

8. Data transfers outside the EEA

No data is transferred outside the European Economic Area. AWS S3 eu-west-1 is located in Ireland. AI Builders API data processing location - please check their privacy policy for details.

9. Changes to this notice

If this notice changes materially (new data collected, new processor, changed retention period), the consent prompt will reappear and you will be asked to agree again. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent change.

How Maya handles your data

A short public summary of Maya's privacy and transparency controls. Full detail follows in the Privacy Notice below.

  • AI disclosure: Maya states that it is an AI system at first interaction (EU AI Act Article 50 transparency posture).
  • Opt-in saving: Conversation logging uses GDPR consent. Chat works fully if you leave saving unticked.
  • Per-chat choice: Each new chat starts with a fresh saving decision.
  • EU log storage: Saved conversation logs and consent records are stored in the EU (AWS eu-west-1, Ireland).
  • 90-day retention: Saved chats and consent records are deleted automatically after 90 days.
  • Server-side consent receipts: When you make a saving choice, a minimal consent record is kept so consent can be demonstrated.
  • Inference may leave the EEA: Your message is sent to an AI provider to generate a reply. That processing may take place outside the EEA, including in the United States, with GDPR Chapter V safeguards where applicable.

3. What data is collected?

If you use the chat, the following may be recorded for each conversation turn when saving is enabled:

  • Your message - the text you type and send
  • Maya's response - the reply generated by the AI
  • IP address - pseudonymised (hashed) in storage; used to group messages and support your rights
  • Browser information - user agent string (browser type and version)
  • Timestamps - when each message was sent
  • Technical metadata - response time, status codes

A minimal consent record (notice version, your choice, timestamp, pseudonymised IP) is kept on our side so consent can be demonstrated. Your saving choice for each chat is stored with that chat session in your browser. A new chat always starts with a fresh consent step.

No account, name, or email address is collected through the chat. No payment data is processed.

This chat page does not use third-party analytics or advertising trackers.

4. Why is this data collected?

In plain terms: processing your message is what makes Maya work at all, while saving chats is entirely your choice. Opt-in saving has two outcomes: it helps Janet improve Maya and her digital solutions quality, and it keeps logging optional, consent-based, and time-bounded at 90 days. We rely on two legal bases, depending on the processing:

Legitimate interests (GDPR Article 6(1)(f))

  • Generating replies: your message is sent to our AI provider so Maya can respond. This happens when you use the chat after reviewing this notice and making your choice.
  • Abuse prevention: rate limits and minimal server security logs (for example pseudonymised IP and timestamp) protect the service.

Consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a))

  • Conversation saving: storing messages and related metadata so Janet can review quality and improve Maya and her digital solutions over time. Saving applies only if you tick the optional box on the welcome screen. If you leave it unticked, no conversation logs are kept. Saved records are held for 90 days only, then deleted automatically.

No data is used for advertising, sold to third parties, or shared beyond the processors listed in section 7.

5. How long is data kept?

Conversation logs and consent records are automatically deleted after 90 days from all storage locations. Short-lived security logs used for abuse prevention are deleted within 30 days. You can also request earlier deletion at any time (see section 8).

6. Opt-in, opt-out, and how saving works

Opt-in is how users who wish to can support quality improvement of Janet's digital solutions while remaining protected by EU storage and the 90-day retention limit. It is never required to use the chat.

Before you chat
When you open Maya or start a new chat, you see a welcome screen. Tap Start chatting to continue. Your messages are always processed by our AI service to generate replies (see section 9). Nothing is saved unless you tick the optional box on that screen.

Opt-in (turn saving on for this chat)
If you tick Save this chat to help Janet improve Maya's answers and then tap Start chatting, you opt in to conversation logging for that chat only. Messages and related metadata may be stored for up to 90 days, then deleted automatically. Please do not share personal or financial information.

Once you opt in for a chat, that choice is locked for that conversation. You cannot turn saving off in the same chat. This is by design so your choice is clear and consistent for that session.

Opt-out (chat without saving)

  1. Click + New Chat on the left.
  2. On the welcome screen, leave the save box unticked.
  3. Click Start chatting.

Each new chat begins with a fresh consent choice. Your choice in one chat does not carry over to the next.

If you already opted in and want to stop saving going forward
Use + New Chat as above. You cannot disable saving in the chat where you already opted in. Messages already saved in that chat remain subject to the 90-day retention period unless you request earlier deletion (section 8).

Status in the chat
Below the message box, Maya shows whether this chat is being saved. You can tap Learn more for a short explanation. This is information only; it is not a control to reverse opt-in mid-chat.

If this notice changes materially
The welcome screen will appear again and you must review it and make your choice again before chatting.

7. Who processes your data?

Your data passes through two processors when you use the chat:

  • AI Builders API - processes your message to generate Maya's response. Messages are sent over an encrypted connection. Processing may take place outside the EEA, including in the United States (see section 9).
  • Amazon Web Services (AWS S3, eu-west-1) - stores conversation logs and consent records in Ireland. IP addresses are stored only in pseudonymised (hashed) form in storage.

Like most websites, this page loads fonts and code libraries from content delivery networks (Google Fonts and jsDelivr). These providers receive standard technical data such as IP address and browser type when the page loads, never your chat content.

No other third parties receive your chat data.

For more detail on how any processor handles your data, contact info@janetxiushi.me and Janet will respond directly.

8. Your rights

Under GDPR, you have the following rights:

  • Access - request a copy of the data held about you
  • Rectification - request correction of inaccurate data
  • Erasure - request deletion of your conversation data
  • Restriction of processing - request that we limit how we use your data in certain circumstances
  • Data portability - request a copy of data you provided in a structured, commonly used format, where applicable
  • Objection - object to processing based on legitimate interests where applicable
  • Withdraw consent - for future chats, start a new chat without ticking the save box (see section 6). For data already saved, you may request erasure (see Erasure above). Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.
  • Lodge a complaint - with the Data Protection Commission (DPC), Ireland's supervisory authority, at dataprotection.ie

To exercise your rights, email info@janetxiushi.me. For an erasure request, include the approximate date and time of your conversation. Because no account is required, it may not always be possible to link you to specific records (GDPR Article 11). If identification is not possible, Janet will tell you.

Requests are fulfilled within one month.

9. Data transfers outside the EEA

Generating Maya's replies involves sending your message content to our AI provider, whose processing may take place outside the EEA, including in the United States. This transfer is protected by appropriate safeguards under GDPR Chapter V, including Standard Contractual Clauses where applicable.

Conversation logs and consent records are stored only in the EEA (AWS eu-west-1, Ireland) and are not transferred outside it. For details of the transfer safeguards, contact info@janetxiushi.me.

No automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects are made. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, with access restricted to the data controller.

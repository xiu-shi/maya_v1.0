Privacy Notice

Last updated: 15 July 2026  |  Effective: 12 July 2026

Plain-English summary: When you chat with Maya, your message is processed by an AI service outside the European Economic Area to generate a reply. This applies whichever option you choose below. Conversation logging (messages, pseudonymised IP, browser info) is optional: you can allow logging for up to 90 days to help improve Maya, or chat without logging. You can change your logging choice at any time using the control below the chat input.

1. Who is responsible for your data?

Data Controller: Janet Xiu Shi
Contact: info@janetxiushi.me

If you have any questions about how your data is used, or wish to exercise your rights, please contact Janet directly at the email above.

2. What data is collected?

If you use the chat, the following may be recorded for each conversation turn when logging is enabled:

  • Your message - the text you type and send
  • Maya's response - the reply generated by the AI
  • IP address - pseudonymised (hashed) in storage; used to group messages and support your rights
  • Browser information - user agent string (browser type and version)
  • Timestamps - when each message was sent
  • Technical metadata - response time, status codes

A minimal consent record (notice version, your choice, timestamp, pseudonymised IP) is kept on our side so consent can be demonstrated. Your choice is also saved in your browser so you are not asked again on return visits until the notice changes.

No account, name, or email address is collected through the chat. No payment data is processed.

This chat page does not use third-party analytics or advertising trackers.

3. Why is this data collected?

We rely on two legal bases, depending on the processing:

Legitimate interests (GDPR Article 6(1)(f))

  • Generating replies: your message is sent to our AI provider so Maya can respond. This happens when you use the chat after reviewing this notice and making your choice.
  • Abuse prevention: rate limits and minimal server security logs (for example pseudonymised IP and timestamp) protect the service.

Consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a))

  • Conversation logging: storing messages and related metadata to review quality and improve Maya over time. Logging applies only if you accept. You can chat without logging if you prefer.

No data is used for advertising, sold to third parties, or shared beyond the processors listed in section 6.

4. How long is data kept?

Conversation logs and consent records are automatically deleted after 90 days from all storage locations. You can also request earlier deletion at any time (see section 7).

5. Your consent and how to withdraw it

Before your first message can be sent, you must review this notice and make a choice. If you select Chat without logging, you can still chat; no conversation logs are kept. If you select Allow logging and chat, conversation logs may be kept for up to 90 days.

To change your logging choice after your first decision: use the privacy control below the chat input. Withdrawing logging consent stops further logging but does not block chat. It does not delete data already collected - to request deletion of past data, see section 7.

If this notice changes materially, the consent prompt will reappear and you must review it and make your choice again before chatting.

6. Who processes your data?

Your data passes through two processors when you use the chat:

  • AI Builders API - processes your message to generate Maya's response. Messages are sent over an encrypted connection. Processing may take place outside the EEA, including in the United States (see section 8).
  • Amazon Web Services (AWS S3, eu-west-1) - stores conversation logs and consent records in Ireland. IP addresses are stored only in pseudonymised (hashed) form in storage metadata.

No other third parties receive your chat data.

For more detail on how any processor handles your data, contact info@janetxiushi.me and Janet will respond directly.

7. Your rights

Under GDPR, you have the following rights:

  • Access - request a copy of the data held about you
  • Rectification - request correction of inaccurate data
  • Erasure - request deletion of your conversation data
  • Restriction of processing - request that we limit how we use your data in certain circumstances
  • Data portability - request a copy of data you provided in a structured, commonly used format, where applicable
  • Objection - object to processing based on legitimate interests where applicable
  • Withdraw consent - at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal
  • Lodge a complaint - with the Data Protection Commission (DPC), Ireland's supervisory authority, at dataprotection.ie

To exercise your rights, email info@janetxiushi.me. For an erasure request, include the approximate date and time of your conversation. Because no account is required, it may not always be possible to link you to specific records (GDPR Article 11). If identification is not possible, Janet will tell you.

Requests are fulfilled within one month.

8. Data transfers outside the EEA

Generating Maya's replies involves sending your message content to our AI provider, whose processing may take place outside the EEA, including in the United States. This transfer is protected by appropriate safeguards under GDPR Chapter V, including Standard Contractual Clauses where applicable.

Conversation logs and consent records are stored only in the EEA (AWS eu-west-1, Ireland) and are not transferred outside it. For details of the transfer safeguards, contact info@janetxiushi.me.

9. Important limitations

This service is not intended for anyone under 16, and is not designed to process special categories of data (such as health or political opinions). Please do not include sensitive personal information in your messages.

No automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects are made. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, with access restricted to the data controller.

10. Changes to this notice

If this notice changes materially (new data collected, new processor, changed retention period), the consent prompt will reappear and you must review it and make your choice again before chatting. The "Last updated" and "Effective" dates at the top of this page reflect when this notice was published and when it applies.
